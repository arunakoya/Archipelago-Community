Closing the deployment gap

Strong climate technologies can stall after investment because deployment depends on multiple organisations acting together. Founders must navigate institutional partners, infrastructure, communities, governance, procurement and evidence requirements—often before they have the internal capacity to do so.

What Archipelago delivers

A neutral coordination layer connecting climate innovators with the organisations and communities required for successful deployment.
